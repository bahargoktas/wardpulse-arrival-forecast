# Data card

**Source:** Fully synthetic hourly aggregate arrival counts, created by NumPy Poisson sampling.

**Shape:** 150 days of hourly counts by default (3,600 rows). Timestamps are synthetic.

**Known shift:** Mean intensity increases toward the end of the period to model a distribution change.

**Privacy:** No real patients, identifiers, protected health information, or external clinical datasets.

**Limitations:** Synthetic patterns cannot establish real hospital arrival characteristics. Not suitable for planning or decisions.
